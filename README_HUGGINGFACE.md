# 地球的熱 Earth_Heat

經典 13 頁地球科學簡報完整數位化 + 詳細中文解說 + 最新台灣地熱資訊  
適合高中、大學地球科學課程、教師備課、自學者使用

## Features

- 📊 互動式地球能量系統視覺化
- 🔥 地球內部熱分布詳解
- 🌡️ 溫度剖面與熱流數據
- 🏔️ 台灣地熱資源介紹
- 🔬 GeoTEX 地熱交換技術

## Content Overview

### Core Concepts Covered

1. **Energy Sources**
   - Solar radiation vs. Internal heat
   - Energy budget comparison
   - Heat flow distribution

2. **Thermodynamics**
   - Temperature and heat fundamentals
   - Units and measurements
   - Heat transfer mechanisms

3. **Earth's Internal Heat**
   - Temperature profile from surface to core
   - Heat production rates
   - Isotopic concentrations (U, Th, K)

4. **Geothermal Applications**
   - Taiwan's geothermal resources
   - Power generation technologies
   - Future potential and development
